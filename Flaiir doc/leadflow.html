FAQ

Common questions

How does LeadFlow find leads?
We scan Reddit (r/forhire, r/freelance, r/hiring), Reed.co.uk, and We Work Remotely every 6 hours. Leads are processed by our AI scoring model before appearing in your feed — so you only see what's worth your time.
How does the quality score work?
Scores are based on budget clarity, scope specificity, response rate signals, and match against your profile. A 9+ lead typically has a clear brief, stated budget, and a hiring decision expected within two weeks.
Can I apply directly, or does LeadFlow intermediate?
We never intermediate. Every lead includes a direct link to the original post. You apply on the client's terms, on the original platform — no middleman, no markup.
How is this different from job boards?
Job boards surface everything. LeadFlow surfaces only what matches your profile, with quality scores so you can prioritise. You spend 10 minutes on the best leads instead of an hour trawling everything.
What happens after my trial ends?
You'll be moved to the Free plan (3 leads/week) unless you upgrade. We'll send a reminder 48 hours before the trial ends. No card is required to start — ever.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes, always. Cancel from your account settings at any time and you keep access until the end of your billing period. No fees, no friction.
What kinds of freelancers does LeadFlow work for?
We cover design, development, copywriting, marketing, and consulting. Our scoring model is tuned for UK and remote freelance markets, with more regions coming soon.
Is my profile data shared with clients?
No. Your profile is used only to filter and score leads relevant to you. Clients on other platforms never see your LeadFlow profile.
